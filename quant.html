Role Focus

At Eurex, I work on market-facing quantitative models that support exchange price formation and downstream risk analytics for exchange-traded derivatives.

The role spans quantitative research, model prototyping, calibration design, and delivery of validated algorithms into production in close collaboration with engineering and risk teams.

Delivery Scope

Pricing and Sensitivity Engines

Design and validation of option pricing and Greek frameworks used in daily exchange pricing workflows.

Calibration Architecture

Construction of calibration routines for rates, dividends, and volatility surfaces with stable behaviour across both normal and stressed market regimes.

Production Handover

Delivery of validated quantitative libraries to engineering teams for integration into production pricing and risk infrastructure.

Selected Contributions

  • Replaced and extended legacy pricing components with internally developed option valuation and sensitivity models.
  • Designed and implemented calibration frameworks that improved robustness and operational consistency across varied market conditions.
  • Maintained core quantitative libraries with tested algorithms and clear implementation interfaces for downstream systems.
